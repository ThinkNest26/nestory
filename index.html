How it works

Test. Read. Learn. Grow.

One simple cycle that keeps every child reading texts that are just right: not too easy, not too hard.

1
🎯

Find the level

An adaptive reading test gives each child an estimated reading age, a book band and a skills profile.

2
📖

Read just-right texts

Their bookshelf shows only texts from their band: stories, information texts and poems.

3
✏️

Learn grammar inside the text

Every text teaches the grammar for that stage, spotted in the reading first, then practised.

4
🌱

Move and track

Children move up or down as they grow. Their texts change, and every step is tracked over time.

The reading age test

A test designed to find where each child really is

The heart of Nestory. The test starts near the child's age and adapts with every answer until it finds the level where they are secure.

  • Adaptive: it gets harder or easier as the child answers
  • Two parts: sentence completion, then passage comprehension
  • Early version with pictures, audio and an adult for FS1 to Year 1
  • Skills profile: retrieval, inference, vocabulary and more
  • Termly retests with three parallel versions
  • No ceiling at 11: strong readers keep going up to 15+

Grammar built in

Children read, then learn the grammar they just met

Every band follows the National Curriculum grammar for that stage, from capital letters and full stops to the passive voice and semi-colons.

  • Grammar spotted in a real text before it is practised
  • Year 1 to Year 6 grammar objectives, Early Years goals for FS
  • Comprehension questions linked to KS1 and KS2 reading skills
  • Key vocabulary with child-friendly meanings
Progress over time

See every reader grow, term by term

Every test, text and band move is saved with a date, so you can see progress for each child, class, year group and the whole school.

  • Reading age against actual age: catching up, keeping pace or falling behind
  • Progress rate: months gained compared with months passed
  • Band journey with dates and reasons for every move
  • Skills and grammar over time, with alerts when progress slows
